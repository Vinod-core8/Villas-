Our philosophy

Villas aren't sold on volume.
They're sold on fit.

A ₹2.5 Cr+ villa is a considered, high-trust purchase. Cheap, unqualified clicks waste a sales team's time and inflate cost-per-sale even when cost-per-lead looks low. We build every Meta and Google campaign around the metric that actually predicts a site visit and a closed deal.

Why it matters

Broad-reach campaigns optimised for clicks fill a CRM with names that never answer the phone. Every setting below — targeting, creative, bidding, funnel — is chosen instead to protect lead quality, because on a villa-sized ticket, one real buyer is worth more than a hundred curious ones.
